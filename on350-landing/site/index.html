How it works

From grandma's card to tonight's dinner in three steps.

  1. 1

    Snap the card

    Photograph a handwritten card, a cookbook page or a PDF. Faded ink and splashes are fine.

  2. 2

    On350 reads it

    It types out the title, ingredients and steps for you. You check every line before anything is saved, and the original photo stays with the recipe.

  3. 3

    Cook, scale and shop

    Make half or triple, add dishes to an event, and get one shopping list sorted by aisle.

Why “On350”

Because so many good things start at 350°F.

Family recipes live on stained index cards, in the margins of old cookbooks and in the heads of the people who make them best. On350 keeps them safe, makes them easy to cook from, and lets you pass them on, with the oven already on.

Questions

Good to know

Is On350 free?

Yes. Everything is free during early access. When Premium launches, you'll see exactly what it includes and costs before paying anything.

Are my family recipes private?

Yes. Recipes you save stay in your private cabinet. Nothing goes to the community library unless you choose to publish it.

Can I trust the allergy labels?

They're worked out from the ingredient list to help you find recipes quickly. Always read the ingredients yourself, especially for serious allergies.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Recipes saved on your phone open without a connection, and changes sync when you're back online.
