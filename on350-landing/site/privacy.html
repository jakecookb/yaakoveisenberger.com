Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information On350 (“On350”, “we”, “us”) collects when you use the On350 app at app.on350.com, our website at on350.com and our mobile apps (together, the “Service”), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and, if you choose, a phone number.
  • Your content: recipes, photos, scans, PDFs, notes, events and shopping lists you add.
  • Usage information: which recipes are opened, saved or rated, and basic technical data such as device type, browser and approximate location derived from your IP address. We use this to run the Service, prevent abuse and, in aggregate, to fairly credit cookbook authors.
  • Messages: emails you send us and our replies.
  • Payment information: when paid subscriptions launch, payments will be handled by a payment provider. We will not store your full card number.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Service, including reading recipe photos you upload and searching your recipes.
  • To send account emails (sign-in links, password resets) and, if you allow it, notifications about cookbooks and authors you follow. You can turn notification emails off at any time in Settings or with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • To keep the Service secure and to prevent fraud, spam and abuse.
  • To comply with the law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your private recipes for advertising.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the Service, under agreements that limit how they may use it:

  • Supabase (database, storage and accounts)
  • Cloudflare (hosting, security, bot protection and email routing)
  • Resend (sending email)
  • Google (reading the text in recipe photos you choose to scan)

We may also share information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

4. Public content

Recipes you keep in your cabinet are private. If you choose to publish a recipe or cookbook, it and the name you publish under become visible to other members.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and your content at any time by emailing support@on350.com. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, email support@on350.com. We will respond within the time required by law.

7. Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies and your device’s local storage to keep you signed in, remember your settings and keep your recipes available without a connection. We do not use advertising cookies.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and other safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will let you know by email or in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@on350.com.
